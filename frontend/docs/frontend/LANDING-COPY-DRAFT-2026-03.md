## 11. Copy Notes For Design

1. The hero should not compete with a large data wall.
2. Room cards should stay short and visual.
3. Trust copy should be calm, not defensive.
4. Avoid overusing the phrase `on-chain`.
5. Keep the landing page focused on entry, not full protocol explanation.
