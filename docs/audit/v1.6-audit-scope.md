This document is the scope statement for the external audit (freeze sign-off FZ-6). It lists what is audited, the
objects of review, the fixed decisions, the earlier findings and the known residuals. Each delivery adds a `SCOPE.md`

## What is audited

- One Base Sepolia (84532) USDC casino pool with one hub, GameHub, and eight casino modules. Sports was removed from
  this candidate (B9); it will return as a separate version with its own audit. Mainnet needs its own certificate.
- Contracts: everything under `src/` except `src/mocks/`. This covers `access/` (Governable, GovernanceTimelock,
  Sentinel), `core/` (VRFHub, GameHub, SettlementRouter, PoolRegistry, Bank, LiquidityVault, interfaces),
  `engines/referral/`, `libs/` and `modules/`. It also covers the pinned Azuro V3 tree in `src/vendor/azuro-v3/` with
  its RANGE_SUM and UNUSED_SUFFIX patches; FixedMath and the interface are unchanged.
- Toolchain: Solidity 0.8.37, EVM Cancun, viaIR, optimizer 500 runs; Foundry 1.8.5. solc and the libraries are pinned
  by digest in `deps.lock` and installed by `script/ci/install_deps.sh`.
- Off-chain: the SDK, keeper, bet index and website (`frontend/`); the release, deployment, certificate and monitoring
  tooling (`script/`); the GitHub workflows.

## Target design

The [constitution](../constitution/SSOT.v1.6.md), the [executable obligations](../constitution/ExecutableSSOT.v1.6.md),
[NFT deposits](../adr/0034-nft-liquidity-deposits.md), [callback settlement](../adr/0006-vrf-callback-settlement.md)
and the [launch-candidate authority, pause, capacity and void design](../adr/0037-launch-candidate-authority-pause-capacity-void.md)
define one target:

- Every deposit creates an ERC721 deposit ID and a tree leaf. Capital enters immediately; withdrawal
  matures after a fixed 86,400 seconds. Successful partial withdrawal relocks the remainder.
  Transfers preserve the deadline. There is no ERC20 LP share or uniform share price.
- Only the current NFT owner may withdraw, to itself. Amount is a fraction of current leaf value
  using 1e12 precision, bounded by free capital L−K. Zero-percent and zero-amount partial withdrawals
  are rejected; a mature zero-value NFT can be closed with a full withdrawal.
- LP cash resides in Vault; pending player principal and fixed liabilities reside in Bank.
  L is tree-root capital, G=L+P, K=R−P. Reconcile both token balances and all debts.
  Accidental transfers are diagnostic surplus, not LP capital or a new sweep facility.
- New positions capture an immutable deposit cutoff and maximum LP loss k=r-s at acceptance.
  Vault uses the prefix for either sign iff current prefix capital>=k, otherwise all used leaves (the
  whole-pool domain); retain the pinned tree's integer operations; k>0 skips the vendor preview (theorem
  in ADR-0034 amendment 2026-10-07), k=0 keeps `_addLimit` with its vendor preview fallback.
  New LPs are not categorically insulated from old bets.
- A successful exit ends all rights to subsequently booked P&L. No queues, batches, recovery claims,
  last-share guard or extra LP percentage withdrawal buffer remain. K>0 prevents emptying capital.
  Empty-root positive settlement is explicitly assigned to protocol capital; no ownerless tree credit.
- Retain the existing G-based new-bet reserve rule and separate PF/XP outflow policy.
  Bound G by 2^108−1, check casts/counters and prove lazy-tree intermediate arithmetic.
- Bank creates and immutably binds its Vault. LP operations use Vault directly and cannot interleave
  with Bank accounting. Internal settlement must not be blocked by the external LP result gate.
- VRF callbacks persist the original seed before attempting atomic terminalization. A failed Hub,
  Vault, Bank, Router or acknowledgment retains the original result and blocks LP funding operations.
  Unknown errors, OOG or invalid results cannot substitute a refund for the result; only a void under
  ADR-0037 §7 returns the stake of a received result whose settlement keeps failing.
- Player cash failures may become fixed-player payable debt. Later payment allocates no second loss.
  Genuine missing-randomness refunds use the accepted fixed deadline.
- Enforce the USDC minimum of 0.01 per round and the global cap of 25 rounds on-chain; retain the one-hour refund
  default and the capacity of 128 open positions per Bank and 2 per player. Normal callback gas must include the full
  tree and two-custody path; the 25-round cap alone does not qualify arbitrary tree histories.
- Governance is a `GovernanceTimelock` from construction: a 72-hour floor, the Safe proposes, the Safe and the
  guardian cancel, anyone executes. Risk-increasing operations wait 72 hours. Risk-reducing ones (deactivating a pool
  or hub, pausing, cancelling an edge change, disabling a game, opening a void window) take effect at once through the
  Safe as `sentinel`. The guardian can pause its Bank, open a void window for its pool and cancel any queued operation
  (ADR-0037 §2, §3).
- A pause blocks new bets and new deposits only. LP withdrawals, settlement, XP claims and protocol-fee claims continue
  (ADR-0037 §4).
- A received result whose settlement keeps failing can be voided bet by bet. A bet becomes eligible 24 hours after its
  VRF request inside a window opened by the timelock, the Safe or the guardian, and for anyone 7 days after the request.
  The void transaction first attempts settlement with exactly 10,000,000 gas. A void refunds the stake as a player
  payable, not the VRF fee, and does not cover accounting-class failures (ADR-0037 §7).
- Consumers authenticate Vault/Bank/asset bindings, use asset metadata for amounts, distinguish
  NFT ownership from money movements and reflect receipt-block capital immediately.

## Fixed decisions

Findings about how these decisions are implemented are in scope. The decisions themselves are not open for redesign.

- Owner economic rules: a 40-level liquidity tree; at most 25 rounds; a minimum of 0.01 USDC per round
  (`casinoMinBetAmount` 10,000); L0/L1/L2 referral rewards; a 24-hour NFT cooldown with synchronous withdrawal; the
  H-1 same-domain allocation rule; `riskReserveBps` 6000 and `withdrawalBufferBps` 1000 (`ownerPolicy` in the domain
  file). The compiler settings above.
- ADR-0037 (Owner, 2026-10-11, D1–D7): an asymmetric 72-hour timelock, governance from construction with no deployer
  handover, an independent guardian multisig, a pause that never blocks LP withdrawals, the void rules above, 128 open
  holds per Bank and 2 per player.
- The pre-inclusion ordering risk is accepted with disclosure
  ([ADR-0034](../adr/0034-nft-liquidity-deposits.md#pre-inclusion-ordering-owner-decision-2026-10-04)).
- A certificate must leave at least 50,000 gas of margin on its main measure, on every network
  ([ADR-0006](../adr/0006-vrf-callback-settlement.md#margin-policy-owner-decision-d2-2026-10-08)).
- Compliance at entry is off-chain (Owner, 2026-10-14). The contracts do not restrict who may bet or deposit.
  Geo-blocking, KYC/AML and responsible-gambling controls belong to the operator's website and operations.

## Objects of review

1. **The ADR-0037 contract changes (B1–B6).**
   - Authority: `GovernanceTimelock` (OpenZeppelin `TimelockController` 5.5; the constructor and `updateDelay` refuse a
     delay below 259,200 seconds), `Sentinel` and `Governable`. Also the function classification of ADR-0037 §3 and the
     same-governance check on `registerPool` and `setHubRegistered(true)` (`ForeignGovernance`).
   - Pause: `Bank.lpAccounting` no longer reports a pause, and the Vault refuses deposits while paused
     (`DepositsPaused`). XP and protocol-fee claims keep only the outflow-domain check.
   - Capacity: `PlayerAccount { uint248 turnover; uint8 openHolds; }` in one slot, maintained by `holdBet` and
     `_applyHold`.
   - Per-game disable, which has no re-enable.
   - Void: `GameHub.openVoidWindow` and `voidBet`. Review eligibility; the settlement attempt in the only-self child
     frame with exactly `VOID_SETTLEMENT_GAS`; the gas check with its EIP-150 term; the recorded failure prefix.
     Then `SettlementRouter.voidPosition`, `Bank.voidBet` and `VRFHub.acknowledgeVoid`, which excludes `acknowledge`
     and lowers the unbooked count by exactly one. The Vault is unchanged: `cost == stake` releases locked capital only.
   - B4 removes the dead `maxLoss == 0` branch. B9 removes sports, so `PoolDomain` is `Unknown` or `Casino`.
2. **The k>0 preview skip in `LiquidityVault._allocate`.** Review the theorem, premises P1–P5, tree invariant I and the
   invalidation clause in the ADR-0034 amendment, re-argued there for B4 and B5. Also review the adapter's dependence
   on the vendor `_push`, `_pushLazy` and `updateId`, and that k=0 keeps `_addLimit` with its preview fallback.
3. **The vendor patch UNUSED_SUFFIX** (`src/vendor/azuro-v3/`). Review:
   - its exactness under P3' for every nonempty lazy range;
   - the known exception (`_remove` on a never-used tree), which is unreachable from the Vault;
   - why `_isNeedUpdateWholeLeaves` is unchanged;
   - the differentials and negative controls;
   - the upstream → RANGE_SUM → UNUSED_SUFFIX hash chain.
4. **First-callback gas certificate v5** ([text](first-callback-gas-certificate-2026-10-07.md)). It was computed by
   `script/certificate/certify.py` on the freeze commit; its seal is `5726389a…`. Review:
   - the composition rule and placement;
   - the environment model of the frame interpreter (`env_frame_bound.py`, `frames.py`);
   - the wider branch domain of the Bank frame and the mechanical token-call topology;
   - the re-derived ledger (the player slot) and the tree DP;
   - M1–M7 (M7 is new), the M4 census and its scope (erratum E8);
   - the reading of the referral and edge parameters (FZ-7);
   - validity, per-network scope and the void reserve.

   The independent certificate review is pending. Until it accepts, the domain file stays `PENDING_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW`
   and release verification fails.
5. **The release verification chain.**
   - The domain file `script/release/first-callback-domain-84532.json`: `certificateSeal`, `ownerPolicy`,
     `moduleCodeHashes` and `coreRuntimes`, which include the timelock.
   - `VerifyCallbackDomainV16` and `CertifiedRuntime.sol`, which compare the deployed runtimes and their immutable
     wiring on a fork, with `gen_certified_runtimes.py`.
   - `verify_governance.py`: it replays the timelock's roles, delay and operations and every governance event from a

   - `V16Snapshot` (Deployed, Release and Live) and `V16Batches`; `ReleaseDigestV16` and `release_v16.sh`.

## Open gates

1. The independent review of certificate v5. The package is `certificate-v5-review-2026-10-12.tar.gz` (`267e4de5…`).
   After the review accepts, the domain file becomes `CERTIFIED` and cites the review.
2. This external audit and its fix loop. A change to contract code or test semantics returns to a new freeze and a
   rerun; a bytecode change voids the certificate (ADR-0006), so it needs recertification, an incremental review and
   the auditor's re-review. A documentation change is recorded as a freeze delta.
3. A fresh Base Sepolia deployment, through the preflight, the gated broadcast and the timelock wiring 72 hours later.
   Then a signed release and the Release Gate `release-ready`, with `audited_source_tree` equal to the tree the audit
   accepts (set as `V16_AUDITED_TREE`). Then the [acceptance checklist](../deploy/v16-sepolia-acceptance.md), including
   the pause, unpause and guardian-replacement drills.
4. Before any value network (FZ-8): the mainnet certificate and M4, a margin of at least 50,000, K0-4 on an always-on
   host with its public channel, the disclosures, a professional audit, the operator and its compliance at entry.

the external audit replaces it.
