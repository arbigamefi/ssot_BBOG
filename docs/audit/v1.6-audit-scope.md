## What changed

In v1.5 the referral engine accrued protocol fees and referral XP against the whole turnover edge. LPs bore the payouts and variance but kept no share of the edge (repository review finding R01).

v1.6 fixes LPs at half of each casino bet's edge:

- `E = floor(U × h_e / 10_000)` is the edge on used turnover `U` at the bet's effective edge `h_e` (at most 500 bps).
- `O = floor(E × 5_000 / 10_000)` is the operator share. LPs keep `E − O`.
- From `O`, the engine pays L0 rakeback, L1 and L2 referral rewards (bps of the base-edge portion `E_b`, together at most 3,500) and any affiliate markup. The rest becomes protocol fee. Every share rounds down; remainders go to the protocol.
- `SettlementRouter` records `h_e` when a position opens and rejects any settlement whose protocol fee plus XP exceeds the operator share. The cap does not trust the hub.
- Payees and the referral schedule are fixed when a bet is accepted. Base-edge changes and markup-cap increases wait 7 days (`EDGE_CHANGE_DELAY`) and anyone can activate them afterwards. Markup-cap decreases apply at once.
- Sports positions open with edge 0 and can accrue nothing.

## Where we want the most attention

1. **Conservation and rounding.** Check `E`, `O`, `R0`–`R2`, the markup split and the remainders for every `U`, `h_e` and schedule within bounds, including overflow at the extremes of `U`.
2. **The Router cap.** Check that no path settles with protocol fee plus XP above the operator share of the position's recorded edge. This includes partial refunds, which allocate on used turnover only, and timeout and invalid-result refunds, which allocate nothing.
3. **Non-retroactivity.** Check that nothing read at settlement lets a later binding, schedule, base-edge or cap change alter an accepted bet.
4. **Governance.** Check the queue, activate and cancel flow: permissionless activation after the delay, a decrease discarding a queued increase, and the guardian's limited scope.
5. **XP accounting.** Check that awards with reasons `REF_L0`, `REF_L1`, `REF_L2` and `REF_MARKUP` reach the right accounts and keep the Bank NAV identity (B1) and solvency (B2).
6. **Code size.** Check whether any reachable change pushes GameHub or SportsHub past EIP-170.

## Accepted behavior (not findings)

- **Self-referral.** A player who refers themselves through extra wallets can recover the whole 35% referral cap: an effective edge of 1.3% at a 2% base edge. ADR-0032 accepts this as the maximum discount. It does not touch the LP share.
- **Variance.** LPs receive half of the theoretical edge and still bear the variance of game results. This is not a return guarantee.
- **Activation.** Anyone may activate a queued change once its delay has passed.
