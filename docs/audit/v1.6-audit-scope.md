## Scope and rules

Solidity 0.8.37, Cancun, viaIR, optimizer 500; dependencies are pinned. Scope includes Bank,
LiquidityVault, the pinned Azuro V3 tree with its documented RANGE_SUM and UNUSED_SUFFIX patches, unchanged FixedMath/interface, SettlementRouter,
PoolRegistry, GameHub, admitted modules, direct-wrapper VRFHub, referrals, SDK, keeper, indexer,
website and authenticated release tooling.

The [constitution](../constitution/SSOT.v1.6.md), [executable obligations](../constitution/ExecutableSSOT.v1.6.md),
[NFT deposits](../adr/0034-nft-liquidity-deposits.md) and
[callback settlement](../adr/0006-vrf-callback-settlement.md) define one target:

- Every deposit creates an ERC721 deposit ID and a tree leaf. Capital enters immediately; withdrawal
  matures after a fixed 86,400 seconds. Successful partial withdrawal relocks the remainder.
  Transfers preserve the deadline. There is no ERC20 LP share or uniform share price.
- Only the current NFT owner may withdraw, to itself. Amount is a fraction of current leaf value
  using 1e12 precision, bounded by free capital L−K. Zero-percent and zero-amount partial withdrawals
  are rejected; a mature zero-value NFT can be closed with a full withdrawal.
- LP cash resides in Vault; pending player principal and fixed liabilities reside in Bank.
  L is tree-root capital, G=L+P, K=R−P. Reconcile both token balances and all debts.
  Accidental transfers are diagnostic surplus, not LP capital or a new sweep facility.
- New positions capture an immutable deposit cutoff and maximum LP loss k=r-s at acceptance.
  Vault uses the prefix for either sign iff current prefix capital>=k, otherwise all used leaves (the
  whole-pool domain); retain the pinned tree's integer operations; k>0 skips the vendor preview (theorem
  in ADR-0034 amendment 2026-10-07), k=0 keeps `_addLimit` with its vendor preview fallback.
  New LPs are not categorically insulated from old bets.
- A successful exit ends all rights to subsequently booked P&L. No queues, batches, recovery claims,
  last-share guard or extra LP percentage withdrawal buffer remain. K>0 prevents emptying capital.
  Empty-root positive settlement is explicitly assigned to protocol capital; no ownerless tree credit.
- Retain the existing G-based new-bet reserve rule and separate PF/XP outflow policy.
  Bound G by 2^108−1, check casts/counters and prove lazy-tree intermediate arithmetic.
- Bank creates and immutably binds its Vault. LP operations use Vault directly and cannot interleave
  with Bank accounting. Internal settlement must not be blocked by the external LP result gate.
- VRF callbacks persist the original seed before attempting atomic terminalization. A failed Hub,
  Vault, Bank, Router or acknowledgment retains the original result and blocks LP funding operations.
  Unknown errors, OOG or invalid results cannot substitute a refund for the result; only a void under
  ADR-0037 §7 returns the stake of a received result whose settlement keeps failing.
- Player cash failures may become fixed-player payable debt. Later payment allocates no second loss.
  Genuine missing-randomness refunds use the accepted fixed deadline.
- Enforce the USDC minimum of 0.01 per round and the global cap of 25 rounds on-chain; retain the one-hour refund default and the capacity of 128 open positions per Bank and 2 per player.
  Normal callback gas must include the full tree and two-custody path; The hard cap is 25 rounds; this alone does not qualify arbitrary tree histories.
- Consumers authenticate Vault/Bank/asset bindings, use asset metadata for amounts, distinguish
  NFT ownership from money movements and reflect receipt-block capital immediately.

## H-1 allocation rule

Vault now chooses the cutoff prefix iff its current capital covers the original maximum LP loss
`k=reserved-stake`, otherwise all used leaves, for either PnL sign. k=0 retains the cutoff and the
tree's own preview fallback. The H-1 correction itself did not change the pinned tree. Review read-only prefix
replay, real Router fees/refunds, multiple cutoff/order transitions, numerical bounds and full cold
callback costs. Pre-correction source hashes and gas measurements do not qualify the new bytecode.
